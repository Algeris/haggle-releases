# Refund Policy

_Last updated: August 20th 2026_

This is the full refund policy for **Haggle Pro** and the **Haggle API** service. We've tried to write it like a person, not a lawyer — please give it a read before purchasing.

If you have a question that isn't answered here, email us at **contact@algeris.com**. We aim to reply within **24–72 hours on weekdays**.

---

## A heads-up before you read

Haggle is built and maintained by Algeris (https://haggle.algeris.com) and integrates advanced speech-to-text engines, AI models, search APIs, payment processors, OS-level audio &amp; screen capture, and more. That gives the app powerful capability.

If you run into an issue, **please report it rather than immediately disputing the charge**. We read every report and fixes typically land in the next update. We'd much rather earn your trust by shipping a fix than have you walk away frustrated.

---

## 1. Haggle Pro

Haggle Pro unlocks features such as the Profile Engine, Resume Intelligence, Context Intelligence, Negotiation Coaching, JD Intelligence, and Company Research. Pro is a digital product, delivered instantly, and tied to a user / device license.

### 1.1 Please try the Free Trial first

The Free Trial is the same product — same features, same experience — and it exists specifically so you can be confident Pro is right for you before committing. There's no upsell pressure inside the trial and no obligation to buy.

### 1.2 Pro is generally non-refundable

Once a license is activated and Pro features have been used, the value cannot be undone. Pro licenses and subscriptions are sold on a **non-refundable** basis.

### 1.3 1-hour grace window for honest mistakes

Mistakes happen — wrong plan, accidental click, second thoughts the moment after checkout. If that's you, please email us within **1 hour of the order** and **before the license is activated**. We review each request individually, and where it's clearly an honest mistake (no activation, no usage, prompt contact) we're glad to reverse the charge.

For the avoidance of doubt, **"activation" means the moment your license key is successfully validated by our license server** — which is the moment you click *Activate License* in **Settings → Haggle Pro** or log in with an entitled account. Once activation has succeeded, or any Pro feature has been used, the order is final.

If you accidentally **bought the wrong product**, please tell us in that same email and we'll either reverse the charge or swap you to the right product.

### 1.4 Yearly subscriptions

Yearly Pro is billed once for the full term. It isn't refundable mid-term, but you can cancel any time and you simply won't be charged at the next renewal — keeping access for the remainder of your paid term.

### 1.5 Voucher and promo-code purchases are final sale

Anything bought with a coupon, voucher, referral credit, or limited-time offer is **non-refundable**.

### 1.6 One license per person

A license is meant for a single user across authorized devices. If you're moving to a new computer, deactivate the license on the old one — that's fully supported.

## 2. Haggle API

The Haggle API bundles AI, speech-to-text, and search quotas into a monthly or usage-based subscription.

### 2.1 24-hour refund window — first purchase only

If you bought a Haggle API plan **at full price** (no voucher, no promo code), you can request a full refund within **24 hours of the original purchase**.

- This window applies to your **first purchase** of a given plan only. **Renewals are not refundable.**
- Each customer is generally eligible for **one refund** under this window.

### 2.2 Voucher and promo-code purchases are final sale

Purchases with coupons, discounts, or promotional credits are final sale and non-refundable.

### 2.3 Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel any time from your account dashboard. Your plan will stop renewing at the end of the current period.

## 3. General Terms

### 3.1 Taxes &amp; fees

When a refund is approved, sales tax / GST / VAT and processor fees are usually returned along with the price. Refunds are issued in the original currency of the transaction.

### 3.2 Duplicate charges

If you see what looks like an accidental duplicate charge, please wait 3–5 business days for authorization holds to clear. If two identical charges posted, email us with the receipts and we will refund the duplicate promptly.

### 3.3 Please email us before opening a dispute

A polite email almost always lands faster, and gets you a better outcome, than a chargeback with your bank. Disputes are costly — fees, processor penalties — so please give us a chance to resolve the issue first.

## 5. How to request a refund

Email **contact@algeris.com** with:

1. Your **order ID** (from your purchase confirmation receipt).
2. The **product/tier** (Haggle Pro or Haggle API).
3. A short note explaining the issue.

We aim to reply within **24–72 hours on weekdays**.
